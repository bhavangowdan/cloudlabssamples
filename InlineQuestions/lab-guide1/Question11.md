## Question
11. Which TWO of the following are advantages of a vSphere Distributed Switch (VDS) over a Standard Switch (VSS)? (Select two)

## Options
Option 1: NetFlow / IPFIX export on uplink traffic
Option 2: Network I/O Control (NIOC) for shares-based bandwidth allocation per traffic type
Option 3: Free with all vSphere editions including Essentials
Option 4: Configuration is per-host, simplifying troubleshooting in small deployments
Option 5: Required for vMotion to work

## Answers
Option 1 : 1
Option 2 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. NetFlow export (Option 1) and NIOC (Option 2) are VDS-only features. Both are common reasons enterprises license vSphere Enterprise Plus or use vSphere Distributed Services Engine.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
VDS requires Enterprise Plus / vSAN / NSX licensing — not free in Essentials (Option 3 wrong). Per-host config is a VSS attribute, not a VDS advantage (Option 4 wrong). vMotion works on VSS or VDS (Option 5 wrong).
