## L4 — Solaris Theoretical Assessment

### Estimated duration: 30 minutes

### Format

This is a **theory-only** assessment delivered through the CloudLabs question portal. **No live lab environment** is provisioned — Oracle SPARC hardware cannot be replicated in CloudLabs cloud. All questions are conceptual or scenario-driven and stand alone.

### Capability Area 1: Theoretical Knowledge (100% weight)

This single capability area tests depth and breadth of Solaris 11 knowledge across SMF, ZFS, Zones, LDOMs, IPS, IPMP, DTrace, beadm, Solaris Cluster, network virtualization (Crossbow), and modernization-to-Linux patterns.

| Topic | Questions | Mix |

| **Total** | **25** | |

### How this is graded

- MCQ auto-graded; Multi-Choice uses partial credit.
- Short-Answer SME rubric-graded within 24 hours.
- Pass criteria: 60% overall.
