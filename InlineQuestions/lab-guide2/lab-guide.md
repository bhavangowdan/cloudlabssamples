## L2 — zLinux (Linux on IBM Z) Theoretical Assessment

### Estimated duration: 30 minutes

### Format

This is a **theory-only** assessment delivered through the CloudLabs question portal. **No live lab environment** is provisioned — IBM Z mainframe hardware cannot be replicated in CloudLabs cloud. All questions are conceptual or scenario-driven and stand alone.

### Capability Area 1: Theoretical Knowledge (100% weight)

This single capability area tests depth and breadth of Linux-on-IBM-Z knowledge across distros, z/VM, LPARs, DASD/FCP storage, IPL, HiperSockets, channel I/O, dump analysis, workload patterns, modernization patterns, and pervasive encryption.

### Difficulty mix

| Topic | Questions | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| zLinux distros (RHEL/SLES on Z) and architecture differences | 3 | Foundational 2 + Practitioner 1 |
| z/VM hypervisor concepts | 3 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 2 |
| LPARs and PR/SM | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 |
| DASD vs FCP storage (ECKD, multipath, HyperPAV) | 3 | Practitioner 2 + Expert 1 |
| IPL process (zipl, parmfile) | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 (Expert short-answer) |
| HiperSockets networking | 2 | Practitioner 2 |
| Channel-attached devices, EBCDIC vs ASCII contexts | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 |
| Dump analysis (zfcpdump, kdump on Z) | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| Workload patterns (large memory, high I/O) | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 |
| Modernization (Linux on Z replacing legacy mainframe apps) | 2 | Practitioner 2 |
| CRYPTO Express / pervasive encryption | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| **Total** | **25** | |

### How this is graded

- **MCQ (Single / Multiple Choice)**: auto-graded by the portal. Multiple Choice uses partial credit.
- **Short-Answer**: SME rubric-graded within 24 hours.
- **Pass criteria**: 60% overall.
