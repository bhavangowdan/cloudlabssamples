## Question
7. Which IBM Z component is the firmware-level hypervisor that creates and manages LPARs (Logical Partitions)?

## Options
Option 1: PR/SM (Processor Resource / System Manager)
Option 2: HMC (Hardware Management Console)
Option 3: SE (Support Element)
Option 4: z/VM

## Answers
Option 1 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. PR/SM is the Type-1 firmware hypervisor on IBM Z that partitions CPC resources (CPUs, memory, I/O) into LPARs. HMC and SE are the management consoles. z/VM runs INSIDE an LPAR, not above it.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
HMC is the management console (operator workstation). SE is the box-level service element. z/VM is a guest hypervisor running in an LPAR.
