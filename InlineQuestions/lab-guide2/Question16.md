## Question
16. On IBM Z, what does the term "CCW" stand for in the context of channel I/O?

## Options
Option 1: Common Channel Word
Option 2: Channel Command Word - the basic instruction unit that drives channel-attached devices
Option 3: Cache-Coherent Worker
Option 4: Cluster Communication Wrapper

## Answers
Option 2 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. A Channel Command Word is the primitive that the channel subsystem uses to drive devices (DASD, tape, network). It is the foundation of Z's channel I/O architecture, freeing CPUs from low-level device management.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
The other expansions are distractors. CCW is the long-standing IBM Z term for the channel-instruction unit.
