## Question
2. What does the term IFL mean in the context of IBM Z hardware?

## Options
Option 1: Internal Failover Link
Option 2: Integrated Facility for Linux - a specialty processor type that runs only Linux (and z/VM) workloads, priced lower than general-purpose CPs
Option 3: IBM Function Library
Option 4: Inter-Frame Logical channel

## Answers
Option 2 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. IFLs are specialty engines on IBM Z that execute Linux-on-Z (and z/VM) workloads only. They do not contribute to MIPS-based MLC software billing for z/OS, making zLinux financially attractive on the mainframe.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
The other options are distractors. IFL is the recognized acronym for "Integrated Facility for Linux."
