## Question
1. Which IBM hardware platform is the supported execution environment for IBM AIX?

## Options
Option 1: IBM POWER (Power Systems)
Option 2: IBM Z (s390x)
Option 3: x86_64 (Intel/AMD)
Option 4: ARM64

## Answers
Option 1 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. AIX runs only on IBM POWER architecture (POWER7 / POWER8 / POWER9 / POWER10). It is not supported on IBM Z, x86, or ARM.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
IBM Z runs z/OS, z/VM, and Linux. AIX has never been ported to x86 or ARM. POWER is the only supported platform.
