## Question
2. Which IBM PowerVM component is the firmware-level hypervisor that creates and manages LPARs on a POWER frame?

## Options
Option 1: PowerVM Hypervisor (PHYP)
Option 2: PR/SM
Option 3: z/VM
Option 4: VMware ESXi

## Answers
Option 1 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. PHYP is the POWER firmware hypervisor. It is the equivalent of PR/SM on IBM Z and runs below the operating system in PowerVM.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
PR/SM is for IBM Z, z/VM is a software hypervisor, and ESXi is VMware (x86).
