## Question
24. On AIX, what is the relationship between TL (Technology Level) and SP (Service Pack)?

## Options
Option 1: TLs are emergency security fixes; SPs are major new versions
Option 2: A TL is a major maintenance update bundling new function and fixes; an SP is a cumulative interim update applied on top of a TL containing PTFs (fixes only, no new function)
Option 3: TL and SP are identical and interchangeable
Option 4: SPs replace TLs in AIX 7.3

## Answers
Option 2 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. AIX servicing model: TLs introduce new function (e.g., 7.2 TL5, 7.3 TL1), and SPs deliver fixes on top of a TL (e.g., 7.3 TL1 SP2). Production environments typically standardize at TL+SP-N (one or two SPs back).

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
TLs are not emergency fixes. SPs do not replace TLs — they layer on TLs.
