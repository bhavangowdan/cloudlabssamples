## L3 — UNIX AIX Theoretical Assessment

### Estimated duration: 35 minutes

### Format

This is a **theory-only** assessment delivered through the CloudLabs question portal. **No live lab environment** is provisioned — IBM POWER hardware cannot be replicated in CloudLabs cloud. All questions are conceptual or scenario-driven and stand alone.

### Capability Area 1: Theoretical Knowledge (100% weight)

This single capability area tests depth and breadth of AIX-on-POWER knowledge across architecture, LPAR/WPAR, VIO server, LPM, JFS2, LVM, SMIT, ODM, errpt, PowerHA, NIM, TL/SP patching, and RBAC.

### Difficulty mix

| Topic | Questions | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| AIX architecture on POWER | 2 | Foundational 2 |
| LPARs and WPAR | 3 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 2 |
| VIO server (Virtual I/O) and dual-VIOS | 3 | Practitioner 2 + Expert 1 |
| LPM (Live Partition Mobility) | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| JFS2 filesystem | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 |
| LVM concepts (PV/VG/LV/PP/LP/VGDA) | 3 | Foundational 2 + Practitioner 1 |
| SMIT / smitty | 1 | Foundational 1 |
| ODM (Object Data Manager) | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| Error logging (errpt) | 2 | Practitioner 2 |
| HACMP / PowerHA SystemMirror clustering | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| NIM (Network Install Manager) | 1 | Practitioner 1 |
| TL/SP patching strategy | 1 | Practitioner 1 |
| RBAC roles | 1 | Practitioner 1 |
| **Total** | **25** | |

### How this is graded

- MCQ auto-graded by the portal; Multi-Choice uses partial credit.
- Short-Answer SME rubric-graded within 24 hours.
- Pass criteria: 60% overall.
