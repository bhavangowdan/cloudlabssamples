## L5 — Mainframe (z/OS) Theoretical Assessment

### Estimated duration: 40 minutes

### Format

This is a **theory-only** assessment delivered through the CloudLabs question portal. **No live lab environment** is provisioned — IBM z/OS mainframe cannot be replicated in CloudLabs cloud. All questions are conceptual or scenario-driven and stand alone.

### Capability Area 1: Theoretical Knowledge (100% weight)

This single capability area tests depth and breadth of z/OS knowledge across architecture, JES, JCL, datasets, TSO/ISPF, USS, CICS, DB2, RACF, SDSF, RMF/SMF, Sysplex/CF, and modernization.

### Difficulty mix

| Topic | Questions | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| z/OS architecture (address spaces, MVS, storage hierarchy) | 3 | Foundational 2 + Practitioner 1 |
| JES2 / JES3 job entry subsystem | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 |
| JCL basics + job classes + procs | 4 | Foundational 2 + Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| Datasets (DSORG: PS, PO, PDS/PDSE; VSAM KSDS/ESDS/RRDS/LDS) | 4 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 2 + Expert 1 |
| TSO/ISPF | 2 | Foundational 2 |
| USS (UNIX System Services) | 2 | Foundational 1 + Practitioner 1 |
| CICS | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| DB2 for z/OS | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| RACF security + SAF | 2 | Practitioner 2 |
| SDSF | 1 | Foundational 1 |
| RMF/SMF metrics | 1 | Practitioner 1 |
| Sysplex + Parallel Sysplex + Coupling Facility | 3 | Practitioner 2 + Expert 1 |
| Modernization | 2 | Practitioner 1 + Expert 1 |
| **Total** | **30** | |

### How this is graded

- MCQ auto-graded by the portal; Multi-Choice uses partial credit.
- Short-Answer SME rubric-graded within 24 hours.
- Pass criteria: 60% overall.
