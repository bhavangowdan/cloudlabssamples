## Question
14. What is the relationship between TSO/E and ISPF on z/OS?

## Options
Option 1: TSO/E is the underlying interactive shell (Time Sharing Option / Extended); ISPF (Interactive System Productivity Facility) is a panel-based menu environment running on top of TSO/E
Option 2: ISPF and TSO/E are unrelated products
Option 3: TSO/E replaced ISPF in z/OS 2.4
Option 4: ISPF is the OS kernel

## Answers
Option 1 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. TSO/E is the foreground time-sharing shell (commands like LISTCAT, LISTDS run there). ISPF layers structured menus, the editor, file manager, etc., on top of TSO/E. Most sysprogs and developers spend their day in ISPF panels.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
TSO/E and ISPF are tightly related (Option 2 wrong). Neither has been retired (Option 3). Neither is a kernel (Option 4).
