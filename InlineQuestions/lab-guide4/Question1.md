## Question
1. Which of the following is the closest concept on z/OS to a "process" on a Linux/UNIX system?

## Options
Option 1: A dataset
Option 2: An address space
Option 3: A volume
Option 4: A coupling facility structure

## Answers
Option 2 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. An address space on z/OS is the unit of execution with its own virtual memory, similar to a Linux/UNIX process. Common types: TSO/E user, batch job (initiator), STC (started task), CICS region.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
A dataset is data on disk (closer to a file). A volume is a physical/logical disk. A CF structure lives inside the Coupling Facility, not a z/OS image.
