## Question
6. Which JCL statement marks the beginning of a job step and identifies the program to execute?

## Options
Option 1: //JOB
Option 2: //EXEC
Option 3: //DD
Option 4: //PROC

## Answers
Option 2 : 1

## Correct Answer Feedback
Correct. //STEPNAME EXEC PGM=program-name (or PROC=cataloged-procedure) marks the start of a job step and identifies the program. //JOB starts a job; //DD describes a data definition; //PROC starts a procedure.

## Incorrect Answer Feedback
//JOB is the job header (one per job). //DD describes data sets used in a step. //PROC starts a cataloged procedure definition.
